\## Executive Summary



The RVA Platform (Resource Visibility \& Attribution SaaS Platform) has progressed from foundational architecture through multi-tenant platform implementation and into Resource Ontology Phase 13A engineering.



The platform now contains:

\- Multi-tenant architecture

\- Identity and authorization framework

\- Resource ontology foundation

\- Temporal data model

\- Event sourcing foundation

\- Audit and attribution controls

\- Idempotent transaction framework

\- Resource revision management

\- Concurrency test architecture

\- PostgreSQL 17 native validation framework

\- Disaster recovery and operational readiness artifacts



The codebase remains in active engineering status and has not yet achieved production release approval.



\---



\## Product Vision



RVA is designed to become the authoritative system of record for organizational resource intelligence.



The platform provides:

\- Resource governance

\- Resource attribution

\- Historical reconstruction

\- Ownership tracking

\- Source system integration

\- Compliance reporting

\- Enterprise auditability

\- Multi-tenant SaaS delivery



Target customers include:

\- Government

\- Healthcare

\- Utilities

\- Financial Services

\- Large Enterprises

\- Regulated Industries
